PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana - Official Subsidy Information & Slab Structure

This is a major Indian government central financial assistance (CFA) scheme launched by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) to promote residential rooftop solar installations across India.

Official Subsidy (CFA) Slab Structure:
1. First 2 kW Capacity: ₹30,000 per kW (e.g., ₹30,000 for 1 kW system; ₹60,000 for 2 kW system).
2. Additional Capacity from 2 kW to 3 kW: ₹18,000 per kW (e.g., ₹60,000 + ₹18,000 = ₹78,000 for a 3 kW system).
3. Systems Beyond 3 kW Capacity: No additional central subsidy is provided. The maximum central financial assistance (subsidy) is strictly capped at ₹78,000 for any residential system of 3 kWp or larger (e.g., 3 kW, 4 kW, 5 kW, 10 kW systems all receive the capped maximum subsidy of ₹78,000).

Key Goals & Features:
- Designed to provide free/discounted electricity up to 300 units/month for households.
- SolarSmart Advisor includes a Payback Tracker that calculates exact net system cost and break-even ROI period after applying the ₹78,000 maximum subsidy cap.
- Always verify local DISCOM regulations and the latest official portal guidelines at pmsuryaghar.gov.in as state-level top-up subsidies may vary.
