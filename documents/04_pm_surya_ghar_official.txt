PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana - Official Guidelines Summary (MNRE, 2024)

Scheme Overview:
- Launched on 13 February 2024
- Target: 1 crore rooftop solar installations in residential households
- Total financial outlay: Rs. 75,021 crore
- Central Financial Assistance (CFA) for residential consumers: Rs. 65,700 crore
- Implementation period till 31 March 2027
- National Portal: https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in

CFA Structure (applicable from 13 February 2024):
- First 2 kW of RTS capacity (or part thereof): Rs. 30,000 per kW
- Additional capacity between 2 kW and 3 kW: Rs. 18,000 per additional kW
- Capacity beyond 3 kW: No additional CFA
- Special Category States (Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, J&K, Ladakh, North-East including Sikkim, A&N, Lakshadweep): Rs. 33,000 per kW for first 2 kW and Rs. 19,800 per kW for additional capacity up to 3 kW

Examples:
- 1.5 kW system → CFA = Rs. 45,000
- 2.5 kW system → CFA = Rs. 69,000
- 3 kW or 6 kW system → CFA = Rs. 78,000 (maximum for individual residential)

Group Housing Societies / RWAs:
- For common facilities (including EV charging) up to 500 kW (@ 3 kW per house)
- CFA at Rs. 18,000 per kW (Rs. 19,800 in special category states)
- Upper limit is inclusive of individual rooftop plants installed by residents

Key Eligibility Conditions:
- Only grid-connected rooftop solar on residential connections
- Installations on roof / terrace / balcony / elevated structures
- Must use Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) modules: domestically manufactured modules made from domestically manufactured cells
- CFA is based on DC module capacity, not inverter capacity
- CFA is given only once per installation. Relocated systems are not eligible again
- Additional CFA possible only for balance capacity up to overall 3 kW if previous subsidy was already availed under older schemes

How CFA is released:
- Consumer applies on National Portal and gets unique Application ID
- Vendor installs the system
- DISCOM conducts physical inspection, signs metering agreement, fills DISCOM Report
- CFA is released via e-token to consumer’s bank account or loan account (within 15 days after all requirements are met)

Other Important Points:
- Vendors must register on the National Portal
- Minimum 5-year Comprehensive Maintenance Contract (CMC) is mandatory
- RESCO model is also allowed (detailed guidelines issued separately)
- No CFA for commercial & industrial (C&I) or government buildings under this residential component
- States can give additional subsidy on top of central CFA
