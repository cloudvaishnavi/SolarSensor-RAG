PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana - Latest Updates (as of July 2025)

Key Amendments (Office Memorandum dated 07 July 2025):

1. Phase-II Transition:
- Applications under the old Phase-II rooftop solar programme that are already at redeemed / inspected / installed stage will remain eligible for CFA if the claim is received till 31 December 2025.
- Applications that were submitted but not installed as on 01 April 2025 are no longer eligible and will be deleted from the database. Such consumers can re-apply under the new PM Surya Ghar scheme.

2. Additional State Subsidy:
- States/UTs can still give extra subsidy on top of central CFA.
- The additional State/UT subsidy amount should be updated on the National Portal.

3. Technical Specifications:
- MNRE can amend the minimum technical specifications prospectively with the approval of Secretary, MNRE.
- Vendors must still follow the latest technical specifications. DISCOMs will check this during inspection.

Core CFA Structure remains the same:
- First 2 kW: ₹30,000 per kW
- Additional capacity between 2–3 kW: ₹18,000 per kW
- Beyond 3 kW: No additional central subsidy (maximum central CFA = ₹78,000)

Implementation period of the scheme continues till 31 March 2027.
National Portal: https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in
